Karis Adeyemi · Lagos

If you're reading this,
you can call me Karis.

You've gotten this far and now I consider us friends. It's nice to meet you. I'm a brand and visual designer based in Lagos, Nigeria, obsessed with the space where strategy meets craft. I work with founders, studios, and cultural brands to build visual identities that carry weight — the kind that age well.

My practice spans brand identity systems, print and editorial design, and packaging that commands shelf presence. I believe every design decision is an argument — and mine are always made on purpose.

I come from a multidisciplinary background. My specialties lie within graphic design, brand design, and making work feel like play. I've gotten the chance to work with brands across consumer goods, culture, and commerce — and I'm extremely grateful for that.

During my free time, you can find me obsessing over type specimens, hunting for rare print, filming things, or eating whatever Lagos has on offer.

Check my Instagram for my latest work. I also share thoughts on brand and design on LinkedIn. Thanks for stopping by — if you liked what you saw, let's work together.
